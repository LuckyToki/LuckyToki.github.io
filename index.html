01 · Context

The problem

A customer complaint revealed that a temperature-sensitive product could reach a hot destination without the appropriate shipping treatment. The issue emerged during a transition to Shopify and affected the checkout workflow.

02 · Diagnosis

The investigation

I tested the checkout journey to reproduce the issue and locate where the process was breaking. The investigation identified a missing geographic condition, preventing destination-based shipping requirements from being applied.

03 · Resolution

The solution

The customer and operational problem became a clear set of technical requirements.

I used AI-assisted development to create and refine Shopify Liquid logic supporting destination, expedited shipping, and cooling or dry-ice handling. An independent third-party technical provider hired by the retailer reviewed the code before use.

04 · Outcome

The result

The reviewed solution was deployed to support more appropriate handling for heat-sensitive shipments.
