Case study 01

AI-Assisted Shopify Shipping Solution

A specialty confectionery retailer needed a safer way to handle temperature-sensitive orders traveling to hot destinations.

Problem

A customer complaint revealed that a temperature-sensitive product could reach a hot-climate destination without the appropriate shipping treatment. The issue emerged during a transition to Shopify and affected the checkout workflow.

Investigation

I tested the checkout experience to locate where the process was breaking. The investigation identified a missing geographic condition, preventing the workflow from applying the intended destination-based requirements.

Solution

I translated the customer and operational problem into technical requirements, then used AI-assisted development to create and refine Shopify Liquid logic supporting destination, expedited shipping, and cooling or dry-ice handling.

Quality check

An independent third-party technical provider hired by the retailer reviewed the code before it was put into use. Employer, vendor, internal rules, and proprietary code remain confidential.

Result

The reviewed solution was deployed in the Shopify checkout environment to support more appropriate handling for heat-sensitive shipments. No unverified performance metrics are claimed.

What this demonstrates

Customer-centered troubleshooting, checkout testing, root-cause analysis, AI-assisted development, Shopify operations, and collaboration with an external technical reviewer.
