The problem

A customer complaint revealed that a temperature-sensitive product could reach a hot destination without the appropriate shipping treatment.

The issue emerged during a transition to Shopify and affected the checkout workflow. The retailer and its external technical provider remain confidential.

The investigation

I tested the checkout journey to reproduce the issue and locate where the process was breaking. The investigation identified a missing geographic condition, preventing destination-based shipping requirements from being applied.

The work connected a real customer experience with the operational rules behind checkout.

The solution

I translated the customer and operational problem into technical requirements, then used AI-assisted development to create and refine Shopify Liquid logic supporting destination, expedited shipping, and cooling or dry-ice handling.

An independent third-party technical provider hired by the retailer reviewed the code before it was put into use.

Shopify Liquid AI-assisted development Checkout testing

The result

The reviewed solution was deployed to support more appropriate handling for heat-sensitive shipments.

No unverified performance metrics are claimed. The project demonstrates customer-centered troubleshooting, root-cause analysis, Shopify operations, AI-assisted development, and collaboration with an external technical reviewer.
